What you're looking at. The coach is a 2D character — in the real app this is Rive ($9/mo, 43KB, mouth driven by the audio). The voice here is your browser's built-in speech so it runs offline; the shipped version uses OpenAI TTS — 200 lesson lines costs about 36 cents, versus ElevenLabs at $264–$1,188/yr.

Note the coach never holds a guitar. No mainstream app animates a hand playing — a hand covers the exact frets you're trying to see. The dots are generated from the lesson JSON, so they are correct by construction and can never teach a wrong shape.
