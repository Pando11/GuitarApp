# GuitarApp — Redline Review, Sept 5 2026

Reviewed against your stated goal: **ship a first version you and friends can
actually use, gather feedback, and grow to 1000 paying monthly subscribers**,
for an app whose pitch is *AI-individualized guitar lessons for different types
of learners and ages*.

Verified by inspection: `git log`, `07-app/**`, `npm run test:app-smoke`
(28/28 pass), `npm run test:playwright` (18 pass / 1 fail).

---

## 1. The headline finding

**The two things your pitch is built on are the two things that are not built.**

### 1a. There is no AI in the app

`07-app/core/chatEngine.js` is a hand-written persona/template engine. Its own
header states:

> `Ban 5: ZERO network.` — and `Ban 6: cites ONLY real lesson data ... Never invents`

A repo-wide grep for `anthropic|openai|claude-|gpt-` finds **zero** model calls
in any runtime module. `sageCoach.js`, `adaptivePlan.js` (1.5 KB),
`practiceRemix.js` are rule tables. What you have is a well-engineered
*deterministic* lesson player with a scripted teacher voice.

That is a legitimate product. It is not the product you described, and it is not
one people pay a monthly fee for — a fixed 25-lesson sequence is a one-time
purchase in the user's mind, and churns the month they finish Lesson 25.

**The subscription justification and the AI are the same problem.** A recurring
fee needs a reason to open the app on day 200. Right now the app ends.

### 1b. There is no personalization by learner type or age

There is no onboarding, no placement assessment, no learner profile, no age
gate, no goal capture. Grep for `learner|ageGroup|placement` across
`07-app/core` returns two incidental hits. Every user gets
`guitar-lesson-01` → `guitar-lesson-25` in identical order with identical copy.

The three teachers (T1 Sage / T2 Ellis / T3) are a *voice* switch, not a
pedagogy switch. A 9-year-old and a 55-year-old get the same lesson with a
different accent.

## 3. Blockers to money (the 1000-subscriber goal)

`07-app/core/entitlementStore.js` says it plainly:

> `STUB ONLY — NO REAL PAYMENTS WIRED.`

Missing, in order: identity → payment processor → subscription lifecycle
(trial, renewal, dunning, cancel, refund) → server-verified entitlement →
receipts/tax → churn instrumentation. That is a real 3–6 week build on its own,
and it must be server-side; a client-side entitlement boolean is trivially
bypassed.

**Do the arithmetic before building more content.** At $9.99/mo, 1000 subs =
~$120k ARR. Consumer learning apps convert free→paid at roughly 2–5% and churn
5–10%/month. To *hold* 1000 subs you need on the order of 25,000–50,000 signups
and a permanent acquisition engine — you are not building a product, you are
building an acquisition and retention machine. Nothing in the repo addresses
either. There is no landing page, no funnel, no waitlist, no content marketing
(the `GuitarApp-scratch/yt-teaser` folder is the only nod to it).

**Also flag:** your free tier decision (tuner + metronome + Lesson 1 only,
"STRICT, owner decision 2026-08-08") is likely a conversion killer. Nobody forms
a habit in one lesson. Free tiers that convert let a user reach a *win* — play a
recognizable song — then charge. Consider free through the first performance
(L5).

## 5. What "better" looks like — the improvement list

Ordered. Do them top down. Do not start a tier until the one above ships.

### TIER 0 — Get it in front of five humans (target: 1 week)

1. **Deploy `07-app/` to a URL.** Static host, one command. This is the single
   highest-value hour in the project.
2. **Transcode audio to Opus/AAC** (~95% size cut), move WAV masters out of git,
   serve compressed. Fixes B4 and makes B1 viable.
3. **Wire lesson audio into the UI** until the Playwright audio test passes.
4. **Add a 4-question onboarding** stored locally: age band, prior experience,
   goal (song / campfire / theory), minutes-per-day. You do not need it to
   *change* anything yet — you need to be *collecting* it from day one so you
   can segment feedback.
5. **Add event logging**: `lesson_start`, `lesson_complete`, `drill_pass/fail`,
   `session_end`, plus timestamps. Local + POST to PocketBase. Without this the
   friend test produces nothing durable.
6. **Add an in-app feedback button** on every lesson screen ("confusing / too
   fast / too slow / broken") — one tap, logged with lesson id.
7. **Delete or archive the 45 root markdown files** into `docs/archive/`. Keep
   `README`, `CONTEXT`, `AGENTS`, one `ROADMAP`. Fix the three dangling
   `CONTEXT.md` references or delete those glossary entries.

### TIER 1 — Make the AI claim true (target: 3–4 weeks)

8. **Introduce one real model call, in one place: the teacher's response after a
   drill.** Input = the *stored* mastery numbers, learner profile, and what just
   happened. Output = prose only. This is exactly what your own Rule 5 already
   specifies ("LLM prose-only, cites stored numbers") — you designed for it and
   then banned the network. Lift Ban 5 for this one path, keep it server-side
   so no key ships to the client.
9. **Make the lesson order adaptive.** Right now `adaptivePlan.js` is 1.5 KB.
   The minimum credible version: if a chord's confidence < X, insert a targeted
   drill before advancing; if a student passes fast, skip the reinforcement
   lesson. This is rules, not ML, and it is enough to legitimately claim
   "adapts to you."
10. **Branch content by learner type.** Same skill, three copy registers:
    kid (7–12), teen/adult beginner, returning player. Start by writing
    alternate `avatarCopy` for lessons 1–5 only and see if it matters.
11. **Give the app an ending that isn't an ending** — the reason a subscription
    renews. Weekly generated practice plan, new song each week, or the
    listening-engine-scored "play this properly" challenge. Pick *one*.

### TIER 2 — Make it a business (target: 4–6 weeks, only after Tier 1 feedback)

12. Accounts + server-verified entitlements (never client-side).
13. Payments (Stripe web, or RevenueCat if you go native). Trial → paid.
14. Revisit the free tier: free through the Lesson 5 performance.
15. Landing page + waitlist + one acquisition channel you'll actually work
    (your `yt-teaser` suggests YouTube — that's a reasonable pick, and it also
    fixes the "where do 30,000 signups come from" problem that nothing in the
    repo currently answers).
16. Retention dashboard: D1/D7/D30, lesson-level drop-off, monthly churn. Ship
    it before you have subscribers, not after.

### DEFER — explicitly, in writing, in your ROADMAP

Godot world shell, Emerald Hollow art, Mystery Mode, song-from-hum, voice
commands, jam session, band engine, style packs, teachers T2/T3, Path A live
duet, encrypted cross-device sync. Freeze them. They are the reward for having
users, not the path to them.

## 6. Where your plan needs to improve — the four sentences version

1. **You are building the world before the lesson.** Ship the lesson.
2. **Your differentiator (AI, personalization) is the only unbuilt part.** Build
   the smallest real version of it and nothing else in Tier 1.
3. **You have no measurement anywhere.** A feedback round without instrumentation
   is a conversation, not a test. Add logging before you invite anyone.
4. **You are planning at a fidelity your build can't keep up with.** Cap
   yourself: one roadmap file, one decision log, no phase-handoff documents.
   If a doc isn't read within a week of being written, it cost you a week.
