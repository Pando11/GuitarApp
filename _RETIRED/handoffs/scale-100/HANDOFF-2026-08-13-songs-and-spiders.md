## 4. KNOWN ISSUES / HONEST CAVEATS
- **public_domain_songs no longer claims "chord progressions."** The original source (folksong.org.nz) has NO chord markup — its pages are lyrics/prose, so any "chord" regex hit was a false positive. Harvesting real PD *progressions* is impossible cleanly there. The spider was REFRAMED to harvest the **PD song catalog** (names + category) — a legal, useful "what PD material exists" signal. The SONGS track still uses OUR ORIGINAL 8-chord loops (legal line), never reproductions.
- **chord_ref was rewritten** from "grab section headings" to "descend into real `*-chord.html` pages and capture diagram/fingering voicings." Now a genuine external cross-check source for our 8-chord fingerings (though `chord-theory-check.js` remains the source of truth).
- **reviews + popular_songs hardened** against Wayback capture-listing (`*`) and UI-nav leakage; both now clean.
- **SONGS progressions are ORIGINAL, not the real songs.** For most, the real song uses the same open chords, so the loop is playable/recognizable — but the real chord *order* and lyrics are NOT reproduced (legal). If Heidi later wants real progressions, that needs a licensed chord source (new decision/amendment).
- **Teaching-copy quality is machine-authored, not guitarist-reviewed.** Arithmetic gates pass; prose is plain-English but unread by a guitarist. AGENTS Rule 8 says that's acceptable for chord correctness; tone/feel remains a judgement call flagged in prose, not a red box.
- **The durable asset is the SPIDERS, not the .jl files** — re-run anytime with the commands above (deterministic).
