# Song seed S2 — "Four-Chord Songs" (G – D – Em – C loop)

**Category:** songs (parallel track; see `05-content/songs/README.md`)
**Chord set applied:** G, D, Em, C
**Reconciled from:** teaching lesson L10 "Four-Chord Songs (MILESTONE)"
**Status:** seed entry — the G–D–Em–C progression covers a huge share of
popular songs; technique origin reconciled, full song list is a later effort.

## What the student does
Loop the progression G → D → Em → C, one bar each, strumming in time. This is
the classic "four-chord" milestone — once these four are smooth, thousands of
songs are reachable.

## Why it matters
- MILESTONE lesson: proves the student can connect four chords fluidly.
- Every adjacent change (G↔D, D↔Em, Em↔C, C↔G) is a pair the practice engine
  already tracks via weak-pair review.

## Suggested drill hook (when songs enter the UI)
- Metronome ladder on each adjacent pair (60 → clean → +5 BPM).
- Then the full loop, 4 beats per chord, at a steady tempo.
