## What the student does
Strum Em for 4 beats, change to easyC for 4 beats, repeat. This is the very
first full song a beginner can play — two chords, both already taught.

## Why it matters
- Earliest "I played a real song" moment of any major app (Lesson 3).
- Lock the Em→easyC change here; the practice engine already counts this pair
  (one-minute-changes, 30/60 gate).

## Suggested drill hook (when songs enter the UI)
- `one-minute-changes` on Em ↔ easyC at 60/min until ≥30 to "unlock" the song.
- Play-along: 4 beats Em / 4 beats easyC, loop.
