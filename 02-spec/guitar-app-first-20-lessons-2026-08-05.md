## PHASE 1 — First Sounds (Lessons 1–4)

### L1 — Welcome, Anatomy & Tuning
- **Goal:** get in tune and make a clean sound; zero fear.
- **Teaches:** parts of the guitar, how to hold it, how to strum open strings, using the **free tuner**.
- **Avatar:** "Let's get you in tune — open the tuner, match the dots, strum. That's a guitar sound."
- **Practice:** tune every session; 2 min free-strumming to build comfort.

### L2 — Your First Chord: Em + One Strum
- **Goal:** play a real chord (Em = 1 finger, easiest) and a steady down-strum.
- **New:** Em (fingers 2+3 on A/D strings, 2nd fret — data-driven diagram).
- **Teaches:** fret-hand placement, thumb position, one clean down-strum, muting strings you don't need.
- **Practice:** 5 min Em + down-strums with the **free metronome** at slow BPM.

### L3 — Second Chord + YOUR FIRST SONG
- **Goal:** a 2-chord song — the retention hook (Andy's #1 lesson = 38M views).
- **New:** easy C / D6/9 (Lauren's 2-finger reduction) OR a 1-finger A-add9 — pick the easiest diatonic pair to Em.
- **Song (exercise-only / PD):** a 2-chord loop (e.g. Em–easyC) — "you just played a song."
- **Practice:** the 2-chord loop, 3 passes, slow.

### L4 — Strumming in Time
- **Goal:** steady rhythm, not just chords.
- **Teaches:** down-strums on the beat (D-D-D-D), using the metronome, wrist (not arm) motion.
- **Practice:** 10 min strumming the L3 song to a click; aim for even volume.

## PHASE 2 — The Core Open Chords (Lessons 5–10)

### L5 — Chord Changes: Em ↔ easyC (anchor fingers)
- **Goal:** switch chords without stopping.
- **Teaches:** anchor/pivot fingers, "air changes" (Justin), lift-and-land together.
- **Practice:** 1-minute changes Em↔easyC (count switches; write the number down).

### L6 — New Chord: G + Em ↔ G
- **Goal:** add G (3-finger 320003) and its change from Em (pivot: index stays 5th-string 2nd fret).
- **New:** G. **Practice:** Em↔G 1-minute changes.

### L7 — New Chord: D + changes
- **Goal:** add D; connect D↔Em, C↔G.
- **New:** D (xx0232). **Practice:** rotate Em↔C, Em↔G, C↔G, D↔Em.

### L8 — New Chord: A + changes
- **Goal:** add A (or A7 easy); connect A↔D, G↔A.
- **New:** A. **Practice:** the four-change rotation; keep the metronome on.

### L9 — New Chord: Am + the Big Four
- **Goal:** complete Em/C/G/D + Am — the "thousands of songs" set.
- **New:** Am. **Practice:** all six 1-minute-change pairs among {Em,C,G,D,Am}.

### L10 — Four-Chord Songs (MILESTONE)
- **Goal:** play the I–V–vi–IV progression (G–D–Em–C) — hundreds of songs.
- **Teaches:** the progression as a loop; first "real song" milestone.
- **Song (exercise-only / PD):** a G–D–Em–C loop. **Practice:** the loop, 5 min, even tempo.

## PHASE 3 — Strumming & Rhythm (Lessons 11–14)

### L11 — Up-Strums + D-DU-UDU
- **Goal:** add up-strums; the most common beginner pattern.
- **Teaches:** down-up wrist motion, muting on the up, the D-DU-UDU pattern.
- **Practice:** L10 progression with D-DU-UDU, slow→comfort.

### L12 — Strumming Patterns Library
- **Goal:** 2–3 go-to patterns (island strum, boom-chick) across a song.
- **Teaches:** pattern switching per section; feel over precision.
- **Practice:** one song, 3 patterns, pick your favourite.

### L13 — Dynamics + "THE Pattern" (Justin)
- **Goal:** make strumming musical — loud/soft, the alternating bass "pattern."
- **Teaches:** dynamics, alternating bass note (thumb), tempo feel.
- **Practice:** L10 progression with dynamics + bass note.

### L14 — Capo Basics (play in any key)
- **Goal:** use a capo to transpose easy chords to any key.
- **Teaches:** capo position, why it works, one transposed song.
- **Practice:** take a G–D–Em–C song, capo 2, play in A.

## PHASE 4 — Real-World Skills (Lessons 15–20)

### L15 — Faster Chord Changes (SPEED BUILDER) ← existing fixture L01
- **Goal:** turn "I can change" into "I can change fast."
- **Maps to:** `05-content/guitar-lesson-01-faster-chord-changes.json` (spider, lift-off, metronome scorecard).
- **Practice:** 1-min change drill, beat last week's number by one.

### L16 — New Chord: E + cowboy set complete
- **Goal:** add E; connect E↔A, A↔Em (barre prep later).
- **New:** E. **Practice:** E↔A, A↔Em changes.

### L17 — Minor Progressions + Dm
- **Goal:** emotional colour with minor chords.
- **New:** Dm. **Teaches:** C–G–Am–Dm / Am–F–C–G (capo for F).
- **Practice:** a minor progression loop.

### L18 — Fingerpicking Intro (Travis pattern)
- **Goal:** a second texture — simple fingerpicking.
- **Teaches:** thumb-on-bass + finger alternation on a chord; Travis pattern.
- **Practice:** Travis pattern on C or G, 5 min.

### L19 — Read Any Chord Chart / TAB
- **Goal:** self-serve — understand chord boxes + basic TAB so they can learn any song.
- **Teaches:** chord diagram reading, TAB notation, how to find a song at their level.
- **Practice:** read + play one new simple chart from the app library (exercise-only).

### L20 — Consolidation + Your First "Performance" + What's Next
- **Goal:** capstone — play one full song start-to-finish; set a practice plan; preview intermediate path.
- **Teaches:** putting it together, a 10-min daily practice routine, streaks as the habit.
- **Practice:** the capstone song + a written 2-week plan; "see you in Intermediate."
