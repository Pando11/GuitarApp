# Beginner Curriculum Sequences of the 3 Biggest Guitar-Tech Apps
**Yousician · Fender Play · Simply Guitar (Simply/JoyTunes)**

*Researched 2026-08-12 via live `curl` fetches of official sites, support/help-center APIs (Zendesk), and Google Play store listings. Every claim is tagged **[VERIFIED]** (from a cited live source) or **[INFERRED]** (reasonable deduction from verified material, not directly stated) / **[UNVERIFIED]** (could not confirm from any live page). Marketing claims are distinguished from curriculum listings.*

---

## 1. YOUSICIAN

**Sources:** yousician.com/guitar · Yousician Support (Zendesk API) articles 201682971, 360001819378, 201558362, 206912609, 115005190525, 360000478477 · Google Play `com.yousician.yousician`

### Structure / Levels
- A guided **"learning path"** organized into **Missions** (groups of songs, video tutorials, and trainers), which are grouped into **difficulty Levels** starting at **"Basics"** (beginner). **[VERIFIED]** (Support art. 115005190525 "How the bass learning path works"; 360000478477 "reset mission progress")
- The path runs on **two parallel themes**: **"Playing"** (techniques, chords, melodies, songs) and **"Knowledge"** (music theory — "how music works", picking songs by ear, jamming). **[VERIFIED for bass; INFERRED that guitar uses the identical two-theme model]** (the guitar site promises "chords to strumming, fingerpicking and more" plus "essential music theory", and the guitar support article 201682971 lives inside the Playing theme).
- Bass-level counts are explicit (9 Playing levels + 5 Knowledge levels); **guitar level count is NOT published on any fetched page → [INFERRED]** that guitar is similar in shape (multiple Playing + Knowledge levels). Website mentions "level up" and "300+ techniques." **[VERIFIED: themes/levels concept; UNVERIFIED: exact guitar level number]**
- Library: "10,000+ lessons and popular songs"; intermediate reachable after beginner. **[VERIFIED]**

### First 5 lessons (absolute beginner)
- Not enumerated by name on any official page. The beginner ("Basics") level is described as perfect for first-timers and includes tuning/setup and the first chords. **[INFERRED]** that lesson 1 = hold/tune the guitar + first single notes, then first chord. (No source lists "Lesson 1 = X".)

### Exact chord-introduction order
- Open **"cowboy chords"** are taught **one by one** in the early levels: the support article explicitly names **Em, E, and Am** in its introductory chord lesson and lists **C, G, Am** as examples; the full cowboy set named is **A, Am, C, D, Dm, E, Em, F, G**. **[VERIFIED: cowboy chords taught early, one at a time; INFERRED: Em is typically the very first chord, as it is the example lead chord]**
- **F chord**: listed among the easy "cowboy chords" (i.e. a simplified/partial F early), while the **full barre F** is an intermediate topic. **[VERIFIED that barre chords are intermediate; INFERRED that early F is the easy shape]**

### Strumming vs single-note melody
- Exercises are categorized as **Chords** (Cowboy / Fancy / Moveable / Barre) and **Melody** (Riffs / Rhythm / Fingerpicking / Melody-Lead / Rhythm+Lead). Chords are presented first in the Playing theme; melody/lead/riffs appear as you progress. **[VERIFIED: category system; INFERRED: chords/strumming precede lead melody]** ("Fancy chords" = Am7, Dsus4, Cadd9; "Moveable chords" = stepping-stone to barre.)

### First complete song
- "From your **first easy songs** to full guitar solos" — songs are integrated from the start of the path. **[VERIFIED that songs appear early; INFERRED that a simplified recognizable song is playable within Level 1]**

### Theory placement
- **Drip-fed via a dedicated "Knowledge" theme** running alongside Playing (music theory, ear-training, jamming). Not front-loaded as a separate course; not avoided. **[VERIFIED structure; INFERRED for guitar specifically]**

### Barre chords / F
- **Barre chords are explicitly an intermediate technique**: "Once you've passed the beginner level, you can start practicing more difficult techniques, such as barre chords." **[VERIFIED]** (yousician.com/guitar + exercise-names article)

### Practice & repetition
- **Separate Practice mode** (song restarts, Tempo slider 25–125%, "Wait To Play" toggle that pauses until correct note, section looping, metronome, "Auto-adjust speed", Mute song). **[VERIFIED]**
- **Play mode** = stars/points/multiplier for assessment. **[VERIFIED]**
- **Review philosophy: "move on, then cycle back"** — advance at reasonable confidence, revisit later. **[VERIFIED]** (art. 206912609)
- **Gamification: streak timeline, badges, activity report, leaderboards/Weekly Challenges.** **[VERIFIED]** (yousician.com/guitar)

### Deliberately NOT taught (at beginner)
- Deep jazz/Classical theory, standard-notation reading (focus is tabs + chord charts), advanced soloing/improvisation, and human/in-person feedback. **[INFERRED]**

## 2. FENDER PLAY

**Sources:** play-support.fender.com (Zendesk API) articles 43004686659483, 44538398193819, 44538454058267, 44538464500507, 44538598249755, 52721676934299, 44538951526555, 44538531716251, 47841097849371, 44538506005147 · Google Play `com.fender.play`

### Structure / Levels
- A personalized **"Path"** made of **multiple Levels** of guided curriculum, chosen by instrument (acoustic/electric guitar, bass, ukulele) **and style preference** (guitar styles: Rock, Pop, R&B, Country, Blues, Folk). **[VERIFIED]** (arts. 43004686659483, 44538454058267, 44538316044059)
- Fender Play TV (a related offering) states **"3 levels per instrument"** with progressive lessons. **[VERIFIED for the TV product; INFERRED app mirrors a small level count]**. Exact app level count not stated. **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Curriculum designed with advisors from USC Thornton School of Music and Musician's Institute. **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538506005147)
- Flexible: can skip ahead / circle back / jump to any course or lesson. **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538600109723)

### First 5 lessons (absolute beginner)
- Verified first step: **"How to hold your instrument"** (fundamentals), then "building dexterity and confidence." **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538398193819) — exact lessons 2–5 are **[UNVERIFIED]** (support does not list them).

### Exact chord-introduction order
- **Not enumerated** in any fetched support/article page. Chords are taught as part of fundamentals and tied to songs. **[INFERRED]** open chords (Em, C, G, D, Am…) introduced early, order song-dependent.

### Strumming vs single-note melody
- Core skills listed: "basic guitar chords → advanced techniques and guitar solos," plus "chord progressions, strumming patterns, fingerpicking, music theory basics, genre styles." Riffs/lead appear at intermediate. **[VERIFIED skills list; INFERRED chords/strumming precede lead]**

### First complete song
- **"after a few short lessons you'll be able to play something new, whether it's a skill, a recognizable riff or a full song."** **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538464500507) → a playable riff/song arrives within the first handful of lessons.
- Lesson lengths: **Chords 1–3 min, Riffs 3–5 min, Skills & Exercises 2–5 min, Song lessons 8–10 min.** **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538598249755)

### Theory placement
- "Music theory basics" included but **embedded in song/style learning**, not a separate front-loaded course. **[VERIFIED mention; INFERRED: drip-fed/integrated]**

### Barre chords / F
- Not stated in support docs. **[INFERRED intermediate]** (consistent with "basic chords → advanced techniques and solos").

### Practice & repetition
- **Practice Sessions** appear **after most lessons**: auto-scrolling tablature, built-in metronome, backing tracks, **Practice Mode + Feedback Mode** (mic listens, gives real-time feedback), playback speed **100% / 75% / 50%**. **[VERIFIED]** (art. 52721676934299)
- **Chord Challenge**: drill for chord-switching (pick up to 4 chords, metronome, timer). **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538951526555)
- **Progress tracking**: progress bars on Level / Course / Lesson screens, "My Path" view, Level bar fills as courses complete, completed courses turn green, "Last Watched" label. **[VERIFIED]** (art. 44538531716251)
- **Daily streak / gamified points:** NOT mentioned in any Fender support article. **[UNVERIFIED — likely no Yousician-style streak system]**

### Deliberately NOT taught
- Spans only **beginner → intermediate** (not advanced/pro). Video-instructor led rather than fully adaptive listening (though Practice Session adds feedback). Limited deep music theory. **[INFERRED]**

## 3. SIMPLY GUITAR (Simply / JoyTunes)

**Sources:** simplyguitar.com/courses · simplyguitar.com/my-courses/pick-up-the-guitar · simplyguitar.com/faq · Google Play `com.joytunes.simplyguitar` · (app earlier branded JoyTunes)

### Structure / Levels
- **"Structured Learning Path"** delivered as **Courses**. The complete-beginner course is **"Pick Up The Guitar" — 146 lessons, labeled Beginner.** **[VERIFIED]** (course page)
- Other courses seen: "Christmas Song Collection," "Minor Pentatonic Practice Pack," and a **"Chords, Scales and Arpeggio Level 2"** course — confirming a leveled course system (Beginner → Level 2 → …). **[VERIFIED]**
- "Within each course, you can mark lessons as done." **[VERIFIED]** (courses page)

### First 5 lessons (absolute beginner) — WEB COURSE (verified)
The beginner web course shows these as its first (unlocked/preview) lessons, in order:
1. **Be Comfortable**
2. **Consistency is key**
3. **Equipment Tips**
4. **Look after your ears!**
5. **What chair to use when practicing?**
…followed by "140 more lessons." **[VERIFIED]** (course page) — note these are **orientation / practice-habit** lessons, not playing yet.
- **The mobile APP** (the actual "app-based teacher" the brief asks about) is described differently: *"Play chords & songs straight away!"* **[VERIFIED marketing]** → the app front-loads a quick win (chord + song) rather than the web course's orientation. **[INFERRED that app lesson 1 ≈ first chord + first song]**

### Exact chord-introduction order
- Official technique sequence (Google Play): **Guitar fundamentals → Read and play tabs → Playing chords → Chord switching → Strumming techniques → tips → technique exercises.** **[VERIFIED description order]**
- So per the stated curriculum, **single-note tabs are taught BEFORE chords**, and chords before strumming. This slightly conflicts with the "play chords & songs straight away" marketing — i.e. chords appear very early but the formal technique ladder lists tabs first. **[VERIFIED tension; INFERRED first chord likely a 1-finger shape such as Em]**
- Free "introductory lessons" are offered (no full free trial). **[VERIFIED]** (FAQ)

### Strumming vs single-note melody
- **Single-note tabs taught before chords** per the official technique list; strumming comes after chord switching. **[VERIFIED order]** (distinctive vs Yousician/Fender which emphasize chords first)

### First complete song
- **"Play chords & songs straight away!"** — a real song is playable within the first lessons on the app. **[VERIFIED marketing; INFERRED app lesson 1–2]** (web course instead opens with orientation.)

### Theory placement
- Theory is **largely avoided/minimal** at beginner: descriptions emphasize "tips and tricks" and "technique boosting exercises," not theory. **[INFERRED from absence of any theory mention across sources]**

### Barre chords / F
- Not mentioned anywhere. A "Level 2" course exists, implying barre chords sit in later levels, not beginner. **[INFERRED not in beginner path]**

### Practice & repetition
- Built-in **Metronome**, **Tuner** (incl. "by ear"), and **YouTube Slow Downer**; interactive player with **loop controls** (set loop start/end). **[VERIFIED]** (site tools)
- App **listens via microphone and gives instant feedback** on correctness. **[VERIFIED]** (Play description)
- **Progress tracking / mark-lessons-done.** **[VERIFIED]**
- **Dedicated "practice/drill mode" like Yousician's: NOT documented. Daily streak: NOT mentioned.** **[UNVERIFIED]**

### Deliberately NOT taught
- Minimal music theory, no standard-notation reading, no advanced techniques/improvisation at beginner, no human feedback. **[INFERRED]**

## Honesty / Method notes
- All three apps **gate the actual in-app lesson content behind login**, so exact lesson-by-lesson names for Yousician and Fender Play could **not** be scraped; those items are marked INFERRED and based on support articles + store descriptions.
- **Simply Guitar's web course "Pick Up The Guitar"** exposes its first 5 lesson titles publicly (verified); the **mobile app** (the product in scope) is described as chord/song-first, which differs from the web course's orientation opening — both are reported.
- Apple App Store pages were non-scrapeable (JS); Google Play store descriptions (server-rendered) were used instead and are cited.
- Fender Play and Yousician support centers are Zendesk; their public JSON article API (`/api/v2/help_center/en-us/articles.json`) returned full article text and is the primary verified source for those two apps.
- Marketing statements (e.g. "10,000+ lessons," "play songs straight away") are labeled as such and separated from curriculum listings.
