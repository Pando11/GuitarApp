1) YOUSICIAN  (Yousician Ltd., Helsinki — founded 2010 as Ovelin)
================================================================================
Model:   Freemium. 1 free lesson/day; unlimited via Premium+ subscription.
         ~10,000+ lessons/exercises/songs; 300+ techniques tracked.
Feedback: Microphone listens while you play; real-time accuracy + timing.
Path:    A single "guided learning path" of MISSIONS grouped into LEVELS
         (the app refers to "leveling up," badges, high scores). You progress
         mission by mission; freemium gates how many you can do per day.

BEGINNER ORDER (absolute beginner -> out of beginner stage):
  Derived from Yousician's own "12 steps to start playing" guide + App Store
  topic list. This is the pedagogical order the app embodies.

  Step 1  SETUP / CHOOSE GUITAR
          Acoustic (nylon easiest for beginners) vs electric; no cables needed.
  Step 2  GUITAR ANATOMY
          Learn the parts, which hand is picking/strumming vs fretting.
  Step 3  TUNING  (standard EADGBE)
          Built-in tuner in app. Beginners stay in standard tuning only.
  Step 4  POSTURE & HOW TO HOLD
          Proper holding, fretting-hand posture, avoiding finger pain (build
          calluses; nylon strings easier).
  Step 5  FIRST OPEN "COWBOY" CHORDS  [VENDOR: App Store lists "chords" first]
          Major open chords: C, A, G, E, D.
          Minor open chords introduced: A minor (Am), E minor (Em).
          (These are the workhorse beginner chords — C-A-G-E-D + Am/Em.)
  Step 6  STRUMMING
          Downstrokes first, then upstrokes; basic strumming patterns.
  Step 7  CHORD SWITCHING
          Transition drills between the open chords; "switch as fast as you can."
  Step 8  FIRST SONGS
          Play along to simple, well-known songs using the chords learned
          (app gives real-time feedback; "progress feels like playing").
  Step 9  READING TABS / NOTATION
          Read guitar tablature and (later) sheet music; explore the fretboard.
  Step 10 LEAD & FINGERSTYLE TECHNIQUES (introduced as you grow)
          Fingerpicking, then lead-guitar techniques: hammer-ons, pull-offs,
          bends, palm muting, slides. [VENDOR: App Store: "fingerpicking, lead
          guitar" listed after chords/strumming.]
  Step 11 MUSIC THEORY / SCALES / ARPEGGIOS / PROGRESSIONS
          [VENDOR: App Store: "music theory for guitar," "scales, arpeggios,
          chord progressions."] Targeted exercises for weak spots.
  Step 12 CONSISTENT PRACTICE & PROGRESSION
          Streaks/badges; Yousician states beginners can "pass the beginner
          stage in a matter of months," intermediate in 1-3 years.

  Topic mastery order stated by Yousician (App Store, verbatim priority):
    "chords, strumming patterns, fingerpicking, lead guitar, and music theory"
    then "read guitar tabs, tablature, and sheet music, and explore the
    full fretboard."

CONFIDENCE: HIGH for topic order (vendor guide + App Store). Mission/level
titles themselves are dynamic in-app and were not individually scraped.

         lessons; 1000+ song library. Personalised path: pick instrument +
         favorite GENRE (Rock/Pop/Country/Blues/Folk). Feedback via
         MatchMySound listening tech (assesses playing, scores it).
Path:    Organised as SKILL lessons + SONG lessons. Skill levels:
         BEGINNER -> INTERMEDIATE (historically "Beginner 1/2/3" style stages).
         You choose a genre path; songs unlock as the required chords/techniques
         are taught.

BEGINNER ORDER (absolute beginner -> leaving beginner level):

3) SIMPLY GUITAR  (Simply / JoyTunes, Israel — makers of Simply Piano)
================================================================================
Model:   Free to start (basic); full course via subscription. Video lessons by
         "world-class music teachers," with REAL-TIME audio recognition: place
         the phone in front of you, play, and the app recognises what you play
         and tells you when it's correct. Works with ANY guitar; pro tuner
         included. No experience needed; all ages.
Path:    LINEAR, chapter-style course (no gamified "missions/levels" like
         Yousician). The app ADAPTS/gates: you cannot advance until the current
         chord/exercise is recognised as played correctly. [Confirmed by user
         reviews: "won't recognize D chord ... can't move onto future lessons
         it is locked without passing."]

BEGINNER ORDER — VENDOR'S OWN STATED SEQUENCE (App Store bullet list, verbatim
priority under "Learn all the necessary techniques to play the songs you love"):
  Step 1  GUITAR FUNDAMENTALS
          Parts of guitar, how to hold, hand position, getting started.
  Step 2  READ AND PLAY TABS
          Learn to read tablature and play from it.
  Step 3  PLAYING CHORDS
          First chords (App review evidence: starts with easy chords, then the
          D chord is a known gate; core set Em/C/G/D/Am as elsewhere).
  Step 4  CHORD SWITCHING
          Move between chords cleanly (gated — must be recognised).
  Step 5  STRUMMING TECHNIQUES
          Strumming patterns; up/down strokes.
  Step 6  INVALUABLE TIPS & TRICKS FOR PLAYING CORRECTLY
          Technique corrections / common-mistake guidance.
  Step 7  TECHNIQUE-BOOSTING EXERCISES
          Drills to strengthen/clean up playing.
  Step 8  TUNE YOUR GUITAR (pro tuner included)  — also used at onboarding.
  Step 9  SONGS  (continuous throughout)
          "Play chords & songs straight away"; fun songs + complete lessons;
          new songs/lessons added regularly. Progress tracked.

  Corroborating review flow: "It really guides you through steps for beginners!
  Helped me tune my guitar and play my first song." and the D-chord lock.

CONFIDENCE: HIGH for the topic ORDER (it is the vendor's own App Store list,
not inferred). The exact internal chapter names are the app's UI and were not
scraped, but the sequence above is vendor-authored.

CROSS-APP COMPARISON (absolute beginner)
================================================================================
All three teach the SAME canonical beginner core, in the SAME rough order:

  1. Setup/tune  ->  2. Hold/posture  ->  3. Open chords (C-A-G-E-D + Am/Em)
  ->  4. Strumming  ->  5. Chord switching  ->  6. First songs
  ->  7. (tabs) / (techniques) / (scales & theory)

Where they DIFFER:
  - STRUCTURE: Yousician = gamified missions/levels with daily free limit.
    Fender Play = genre-personalised video "skills + songs" path, trial-gated.
    Simply Guitar = linear adaptive video chapters, recognition-gated per step.
  - READING MUSIC: Yousician and Simply Guitar teach TABS early (Simply lists
    "read and play tabs" as Step 2). Fender Play teaches tabs inside songs/
    practice mode rather than as a discrete early step.
  - FEEDBACK ENGINE: All three use mic recognition; Yousician & Simply give
    continuous note-level feedback; Fender uses MatchMySound for scored feedback.
  - FREE TIER: Yousician = 1 lesson/day free (permanent freemium).
    Simply Guitar = free to start, then subscription. Fender Play = 14-day
    trial only (no permanent free tier).
  - THEORY DEPTH: Yousician goes furthest into explicit music theory / scales /
    arpeggios as a curriculum pillar. Fender bundles theory into genre songs.
    Simply keeps theory light ("tips & tricks," boosting exercises).
