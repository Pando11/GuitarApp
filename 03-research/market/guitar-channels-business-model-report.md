## 1. TrueFire — youtube.com/@TrueFire (channel UCiReSwhx7y_LpNFNg4GCuCQ) · truefire.com

Not a personality channel: 400+ signed educators (Steve Vai, Larry Carlton, Lindsay Ell, Walter Trout) each front a "course". Pacing is instructor-led, demonstration-then-breakdown.

**Curriculum order** — Organized by course/educator/genre on the site, not by YouTube playlist progression. Beginner tracks exist ("Jump Start", "How to Play Acoustic Guitar – Lessons for Beginners"); the site sorts by genre (20+), skill level and educator (400+). YouTube is the top-of-funnel sampler.

## 2. Your Guitar Academy (YGA) — youtube.com/@yga (UCFIYhTUPEtQ5K5_rb1-yNmQ) · yourguitaracademy.com / guitarclub.io

**Teaching style** — UK teacher-fronted (Dan Holton + mentor team: Franco, Kiall, Jed, Claire, Thomas). Clean studio, talking-head + fretboard/hand close-ups, on-screen chord diagrams and tab, friendly step-by-step pacing aimed at true beginners. Multiple tutors = multiple styles.
**Lesson setup** — Song lesson or skill lesson: play-through → chords/riff breakdown part by part → slow demo → practice tip → CTA to the matching Guitar Club course/free blog lesson.
**Curriculum order** — The most explicitly structured of the group: the **30 Day Beginner Challenge** (Day 1…Day 30) is the entry ramp, then a "pathways" system with levels (Beginner → Improver → Intermediate) across categories: Challenges, Guitar Skills, Masterclasses, Player Studies, Theory Lab, Song Jukebox (e.g. Open Chords → Rhythm L1 → Barre Chords → Bends L1/L2 → CAGED → Triads → Speed Building L1/L2).

# Cross-Channel Synthesis

### (a) Dominant monetization patterns
1. **YouTube = top-of-funnel, never the business.** All five treat free video as audience acquisition; revenue sits on owned platforms.
2. **Two archetypes:**
   - *Subscription/library* — TrueFire, YGA (Guitar Club), Music Is Win (No Cover Charge). Recurring revenue, big libraries, community + tools, free trials (7–14 days).
   - *Lifetime course ladder* — Paul Davids, Signals Music Studio. High-ticket one-off purchases ($45–$270), upsell to the next course rather than retain a subscription.
3. **Email/community as the retention moat** — Paul Davids' 145K "Inner Circle"; YGA's Discord (free forever) + mentors + live workshops; TrueFire forums; NCC "Backstage" forum + podcast + gear marketplace.
4. **Secondary revenue:** AdSense on huge view counts (Paul Davids 520M, Music Is Win 26M on one video), gear sponsorships/affiliates (strongest at Music Is Win and Paul Davids), merch, side products (Signals' BandAlert, YGA's in-person/Zoom tutor network), celebrity-educator collabs as marketing (TrueFire).
5. **Free-trial + refund guarantee is standard** — 14 days (TrueFire, Paul Davids guarantee), 7 days no card (YGA), 30-day refund (YGA).

### (b) Typical price ranges
- **Monthly subscriptions: $19.99–$29.99/mo** (NCC $19.99; TrueFire & Guitar Club both $29.99).
- **Annual subscriptions: ~$96–$200/yr** (TrueFire $95.88 intro / $99 renew; Guitar Club $169; NCC $199.99) → effective **$8–$17/mo**, i.e. 45–73% off monthly.
- **Lifetime courses: $45–$270** (Signals $45–$270; Paul Davids $129–$199).

### (c) Free YouTube vs paid
| | Free YouTube | Paid |
|---|---|---|
|Content|Standalone lessons, song breakdowns, entertainment, theory explainers|Full sequenced libraries (TrueFire 85,000+ lessons; NCC 500+ masterclasses)|
|Structure|None or loose playlists|Levelled pathways/roadmaps (YGA pathways, PD 7-level, RRG L1→L2)|
|Materials|Rarely tabs/PDFs|Interactive tab, notation, printable PDFs, jam/backing tracks|
|Tools|YouTube player only|Loop/slow-down interactive players, progress tracking, toolbox/tuner apps|
|Human contact|Comments|Mentor feedback, live weekly workshops, Discord/forums|
|Ads|Ad-supported|Ad-free|
|Extras|—|Member discounts (TrueFire 25% off), monthly bonus courses, gear marketplace, podcast|

**Verified-price note:** TrueFire, Guitar Club, Paul Davids and Signals prices were read directly off their live pages today. No Cover Charge's $19.99/$199.99 comes from the App Store listing (its web pricing page renders behind JS and returned empty); treat that one as high-confidence but second-hand.
