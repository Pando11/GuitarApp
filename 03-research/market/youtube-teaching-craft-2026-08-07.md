# YouTube Teaching Craft — How the Top 5 Beginner-Guitar Channels Work
Date: 2026-08-07 · For: GuitarApp YouTube strategy (AMENDMENT-05 §5) · Plain-English owner report.

## Verified numbers that matter
- Andy Guitar "Guitar Lesson 1 – Absolute Beginner? Start Here!" ~38M views; "Play 10 Songs
  with 2 Chords" cited as the most-watched guitar lesson in YouTube history. Funnel: free
  10-day course → paid "Access All Areas" membership.
- Marty Music "Your Very First Guitar Lesson" 14M views (Lesson 2: 4.6M, Lesson 3: 3M).
  Membership $24.99/mo / $125/yr. ~5M subs, near-daily uploads.
- JustinGuitar ~1.9M subs; 1M+ registered free students on his site; gives the course away,
  sells structure/tools. Numbered course = binge behavior.
- Lauren Bateman ~600K subs; niche = absolute beginners skewing 40+/older adults, most
  underserved segment; pain-point titles ("Can't Change Chords Fast Enough?", top ~1.4M+);
  funnel = free "Play Songs in 14 Days" crash course → paid "7 Level Guitar System".
- GuitarZero2Hero: annotated-TAB song tutorials (~1.9M+ each); the screen is the teacher,
  not the face. Funnel = Patreon + courses; pure search traffic.

## The 10 rules for OUR 3–6 min animated videos
1. **Lead with the result.** First 10 seconds: hear/see the thing you'll play by the end.
2. **A number in the title.** "Your First Song in 5 Minutes (2 Chords)". Numbers + "easy" win clicks.
3. **Own the "Very First" moment.** One definitive "Absolute Beginner — Start Here (Animated)" flagship.
4. **The screen is the teacher.** Animated fretboard/chord boxes on screen at all times; never make the viewer pause to read. (This is our structural advantage over talking heads.)
5. **One win per video.** One chord, one change, one strum. End on the win, not homework.
6. **Number the path.** "Step 3 of 10" on every thumbnail → binge pull + return visits.
7. **Play-along is non-negotiable.** Last 60s: "now you try with me" at slow tempo — and the bridge to the app: "the app listens and tells you if you got it."
8. **Title the quit-moments (Lauren's formula).** "Can't change chords fast enough?" ranks forever and converts emotionally.
9. **Sell the path, not the lesson.** End-card CTA: "The video shows you what to do; the app coaches you while you do it." One CTA, benefit-led, never mid-lesson.
10. **Thumbnail = promise + ease + distinctiveness.** Animated character + big "2 CHORDS" text vs. the sea of bearded men with acoustics. Distinctiveness alone lifts click-through.

**Synthesis:** hook with the finished result → teach one tiny win → make the viewer play
along → sell the structured path. Our format is naturally better at rules 1, 4, 7;
we borrow their titles, numbering, and funnel discipline.
