## 1. What the top sources actually do (order)

### JustinGuitar — Justin Sandercoe (2M subs)
- **Grade 1 ladder (methodical, "why first"):** M0 Basics (tuning, chord boxes, pick hold) → M1 **A & D chords + first song** → M2 rhythm & chord changes + first riff → M3 capo, minor chords, up-strums → M4 metronome, stretches, "THE pattern", Dm → M5 basic theory, C chord → M6 6:8 time, G-chord hack, alternate picking → M7 air changes, dynamics, consolidation. (~75 lessons.)
- **Retention device:** numbered, graded curriculum with explicit "next lesson." Practice-routine builder.
- **Signature:** rigour on *technique order* — teaches A & D first (easy, then anchor-finger changes), introduces C later (harder), G via a "hack."

### Andy Guitar — Andy Crowley (3.14M subs; top beginner channel)
- **Free 10-Day Starter:** L1 Start Here → L2 easy 2-chord song + lead → L3 'Three Little Birds' → … → L7 "100s of songs with 4 chords."
- **Paid Beginner Acoustic pathway (L0–L9):** L0 tuning/restringing/chord boxes/TAB → L1 first chords & songs → L2 three-chord songs & strumming → L3 G + common progressions → L4 most common progressions & strumming → L5 theory catch-up + halfway consolidation → L6 beginner blues/busker chords → L7 acoustic embellishments & key of C → L8 fingerstyle intro, minor keys, A-minor pentatonic → L9 consolidation.
- **Retention device:** day-numbered "10-Day" streak loop; 0→100% progress bars; weekly live Q&A.
- **Signature:** "play a song TODAY" framing — song outcome before technique depth. Huge acquisition hook (Lesson 1 = 38M views).

### Yousician (app, 20M MAU)
- **Learning path:** Basics → Chord & Rhythm (cowboy, moveable, barre chords; strumming, riffs) → Melody → Lead. Gamified, leveled, instant audio feedback (real-time, not async).
- **Signature:** structured levels + game feedback. Order: chords/rhythm before melody/lead.

### Fender Play (app)
- **Structure:** Path (genre/instrument) → Level → Course → Activity. Forced onboarding quiz → assigns a Path. Video-led, song-based.
- **Signature:** song-as-the-unit; structured onboarding improves completion.

### Marty Music / Lauren Bateman (YouTube, song/chord-first)
- **Marty:** starts with first chords (A, G), "4 chords" lesson, easy acoustic songs. Chord-first, song-outcome.
- **Lauren Bateman:** Em + D6/9 (2-finger easy C) as "first 4 chords"; dedicated *faster chord changes* lesson (our existing L01 fixture mirrors this). Tension/relaxation + metronome-as-scorecard framing.

## 2. Consensus ladder (what EVERY good source agrees on)

1. **Setup first:** how to hold, tune (our free tuner), parts, how to strum open strings — before any chord.
2. **Easiest chord first:** Em (1-finger) or a 2-finger easy C/D6/9. Get a *sound* immediately.
3. **One strum + one song fast:** down-strums → a 2-chord song in the first 1–3 lessons (Andy's #1 hook).
4. **Chord changes before more chords:** anchor fingers / pivot points / 1-minute changes — the #1 beginner stall point.
5. **The open-chord set:** Em, C (or D6/9), G, D, A, Am — the "thousands of songs" set.
6. **Strumming patterns + up-strums** before theory.
7. **4-chord pop progressions (I–V–vi–IV / G–D–Em–C)** → unlock hundreds of songs. Major milestone.
8. **Capo + dynamics + light theory** to transpose and add feel.
9. **Minor chords (Dm, Am progressions) + fingerpicking intro** for texture.
10. **Consolidation + "read any chord chart/TAB"** so they can self-serve afterward.

## 3. Retention insights we should steal
- **Numbered, sequential lessons with an explicit "next"** (Justin) — the single strongest retention device.
- **Fast first-song win** (Andy) — acquisition + early retention. We open with a 2-chord song, not technique theory.
- **Daily/streak loop** (Andy 10-Day) — our streaks feature maps here.
- **Practice prescription per lesson** (Justin's routine builder) — each lesson ends with a concrete, timed drill.
- **One clean subscription** (Andy's single £10.99/mo vs Justin's fragmented stack) — our $12/mo, one number.
