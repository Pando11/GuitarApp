# Marketing Playbook for the Guitar Lesson App
*Source: "Marketing Against the Grain" Ep 441 — Liquid Death's Viral Ads Masterclass (Benoit Vatere, Chief Media Officer, Liquid Death). Mapped to the guitar-app context.*

## The episode in one paragraph
Liquid Death's media chief breaks down the *system* under the viral brand: audit every dollar by funnel stage, measure **incrementality** (new-to-brand), only swing for home runs (20%+ gains, ignore 5% tweaks), match the **right channel to the right funnel stage** (social ≠ awareness; CTV/radio/podcasts = awareness; Meta = consideration; Amazon/Walmart/Target = conversion), run **different creative for every funnel stage**, pick one creative "lane" (theirs = comedy), and *never let the algorithm pick your creative*.

---

## 1. The core framework (steal this)

### A. Full-funnel audit — "where is every dollar going?"
Most consumer brands over-spend at the top; most B2B over-spend at the bottom. **Map every guitar-app marketing dollar to a funnel stage:**
- **Awareness** — they've never heard of you
- **Consideration** — intrigued, comparing you vs Yousician/JustinGuitar app
- **Conversion** — ready to subscribe

Action: before spending a cent, list every channel you use and tag it A / C / Conv. If 90% sits in one bucket, rebalance.

### B. Incrementality over ROAS — "new-to-brand" is the only honest signal
ROAS lies. Platforms (Meta) claim credit for sales that would've happened anyway. Vatere's cheap proxy: **high ROAS + high % of NEW users = probably real incremental growth.** Otherwise it's fake.
- For the app: track **% of new subscribers who had never downloaded a guitar app / never watched your YouTube** vs returning. If your "best" ad just re-converts existing fans, it's not growth.
- **Geo holdout test (the fast version):** run paid push in City A, nothing in similar City B. If installs rise in A and not B → it works. Don't wait 2 years for a model. (For an app: compare two similar metro/region ad campaigns, or a holdout audience.)

### C. Swing for home runs, not singles
Only run tests with **high upside**. If a test isn't conclusive in ~4 days, the effect is too small — kill it. Chase 20%+ lifts; ignore 5% tweaks.
- App implication: don't A/B-test button colors for months. Test **big bets** — a new creative concept, a new channel, a new hook. Either it moves the needle fast or you move on.

### D. Predictability vs Magnitude 2×2 — be in "max upside," not "predictable low gain"
Early-stage brands should maximize upside, not optimize a predictable 2%. (The 2% game is for 50-year-old incumbents protecting share.)

### E. Channel ↔ funnel-stage matching (the big one)
| Funnel stage | Right channels | Wrong channels |
|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** (need HIGH FREQUENCY — several touches/week) | CTV, radio, podcasts, YouTube (long-form), Shorts at scale | Social feeds (Meta won't let you control frequency; 1–1.5x/week buried in 1,000 posts = nobody remembers you) |
| **Consideration** (optimize for click-out, retargeting) | Meta/Instagram (retargeting), YouTube pre-roll, search, Reddit guitar communities | — |
| **Conversion** (where the purchase happens) | App Store/Play Store optimization, your owned site, retail/digital shelves, email to warm leads | — |

**Key insight for you:** "Social is NOT an awareness channel, period." If you spray TikTok/Instagram hoping for awareness, you're wasting it — use social for *consideration/retargeting* (people who already saw you on YouTube/podcasts).

### F. Different creative for EVERY funnel stage
- **Awareness creative** = remember the NAME and the CATEGORY, nothing else. ("A guitar app that watches your hands and tells you if your chord is wrong.") No pricing, no feature dump.
- **Consideration creative** = why it's better (camera vs Yousician's audio-only; plays a real song in minutes; verified-shape gating). Link brand → benefit.
- **Conversion creative** = price, trial, "start free," risk reversal.
- **Do NOT** try to make one funny/buzzy video also explain pricing and features. It can't.

### G. Pick ONE creative lane
Liquid Death picked **comedy**. For a guitar app, pick your lane and own it:
- Candidate lanes: "The honest teacher who finally sees your hands" (radical transparency / anti-BS), "Watch-your-hands magic" (demo wow-factor), "First song in 10 minutes" (speed/instant gratification).
- Action: choose ONE and make every awareness asset speak in that voice.

### H. Never let the algorithm pick your creative
Meta's "throw 50 creatives, algo picks" dilutes your full-funnel story. **You** decide what creative maps to what stage. Keep consistency of message across the funnel yourself.

### I. Give up control on DISTRIBUTION, keep control on MEDIA BUY
- Let go of "everyone must land on MY website." Distribute where the scale is (app stores, YouTube, podcasts, maybe retail/partner channels).
- But keep tight control over *how you buy media* and *what creative runs* — platforms' incentives ≠ yours.

## 2. Applied to the guitar app — a concrete plan

**Awareness (frequency-driven, NOT social feeds):**
- YouTube long-form + Shorts: this is where guitar learners already are (our teardown showed Justin/Andy/Marty own millions of views). A "camera checks your chord shape" demo Short is pure awareness fuel.
- Podcast sponsorships: music/creator/DIY podcasts (cheaper than CTV, high-frequency to a relevant audience).
- The "wow" demo video (camera overlay turning a red dot green) = the awareness creative. Name + category only.

**Consideration (Meta retargeting + search + communities):**
- Retarget everyone who watched the demo with "why camera beats audio — see the 3 mistakes it catches."
- Reddit (r/guitarlessons, r/Guitar), guitar forums, comparison content vs Yousician/Justin app.
- Search ads on "guitar lesson app," "learn guitar fast," "Yousician alternative."

**Conversion (app stores + email + free trial):**
- App Store Optimization (screenshots showing the camera verdict), a clean ~$12–15/mo or ~$99–130/yr price (from teardown), 7–14 day trial, 30–60 day refund (incumbents all do this).
- Email capture on the awareness demo → nurture → trial.

**Measurement that matters:**
- New-to-app % (real incrementality), not raw installs.
- Cost per product-page-view (here = cost per app-store-listing-visit / cost per trial-start), not just CTR. High CTR on high CPM = still expensive.
- Geo/audience holdout tests on big bets only.

## 3. What NOT to do (anti-patterns from the episode)
- Don't optimize for ROAS alone — it's fake without incrementality.
- Don't use social feeds for awareness — use them for consideration/retargeting.
- Don't make one creative do the whole funnel.
- Don't let Meta's algo choose your creative.
- Don't chase 5% wins — swing for 20%+ or move on.
- Don't hoard control of distribution; scale where the users are.
