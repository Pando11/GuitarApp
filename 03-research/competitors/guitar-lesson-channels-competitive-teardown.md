# Free YouTube Guitar-Lesson Channels — Competitive Teardown
*Compiled 2026-08-04 from 4 parallel research agents. View counts verified live (YouTube "Popular" sort) where noted; paid prices verified on live pricing pages where noted. FX: £1 ≈ $1.27.*

This report answers 9 questions across 10 channels: teaching style, lesson setup, curriculum order, most-viewed videos, advertising, retention, paid plan (yes/no + price + what's different), plus a chord-shape/verification gap analysis relevant to a camera-based chord-check app.

## PART A — ANSWERS TO THE 9 QUESTIONS (synthesized)

### 1. Teaching style (camera / pacing / graphics / hand close-ups)
- **JustinGuitar** — Studio talking-head + over-the-shoulder/top-down fretboard cam + strumming-hand close-up. Calm, methodical, "why before how." On-screen chord boxes + TAB on newer videos. Short legacy lessons (4–8 min), newer 10–30 min.
- **Andy Guitar** — Front mid-shot + mirrored/over-the-shoulder fretboard inserts, picture-in-picture. Chatty, "play a song today." Heavy on-screen chord boxes, scrolling lyric/chord text, count-in countdowns for play-along. 12–18 min.
- **Marty Music** — Single front-cam "talking to you" + intercut hand close-ups. Warm, jargon-light. Minimal on-screen graphics (tabs/PDFs are off-platform lead magnets). 7–20 min.
- **GuitarLessons365 (Carl Brown)** — Studio multi-cam (overhead fret-hand + picking-hand + head). Note-for-note, played fast then slowed. Most technical/longest (30–58 min).
- **Groovy Guitar Dude (Cody)** — Straight-on shot, Shorts + 1–8 min longform, capo/chord info on screen, high-energy, "quick win." Beginner pop-chart focus.
- **TrueFire** — Studio multi-cam, synced on-screen tab/notation. 400+ celebrity educators (Steve Vai, etc.), not a personality channel.
- **Your Guitar Academy (YGA)** — Clean studio, talking-head + fretboard/hand close-ups, on-screen diagrams/TAB, friendly step-by-step, multiple tutors.
- **Paul Davids** — Cinematic multi-cam, colour-graded, animated tab overlays, music-video b-roll. Highest production values.
- **Music Is Win (Tyler Larson)** — Entertainment-first: comedy sketches, gear stunts; lessons are fast, shred/technique-oriented with hand close-ups + split-screen tab. Least classroom-like.
- **Signals Music Studio (Jake Lizzio)** — Whiteboard/graphics-heavy theory explainer; guitar + on-screen fretboard/DAW diagrams; conversational, "why it works."

### 2. Lesson setup (typical structure)
- **Justin/Andy/YGA/TrueFire**: Hook → why-it-matters → slow demo w/ fretboard close-up → common-mistakes callout → practice prescription → song application → recap + "next lesson" pointer.
- **Marty**: Intro/hook → what-you'll-learn → full-part demo → chunk-by-chunk slow → play-along → recap + CTA to free course/PDF.
- **Groovy**: Explicit chapters — Preview Jam → Intro → Capo/Chords → Progression & Strumming → Playthrough → Outro (full loop in ~2–4 min).
- **GuitarLessons365**: Full-speed performance → part-by-part breakdown (Pt.1/2/3) → slow repeat w/ close-ups → backing-track play-along.
- **Paul Davids**: Hook/performance → story/concept framing → demo w/ animated tab → exercise → soft CTA.
- **Music Is Win**: Comedy premise → demo → technique/theory nugget → CTA to platform.
- **Signals**: Premise/question → theory w/ diagrams → real song examples → demo → application.

### 3. Curriculum order (beginner progression)
- **Consensus open-chord sequence**: E/Em → A → D → (Am, Dm) → C → G → **F/barre deliberately last.** Justin uniquely starts **A + D** (fastest 2-chord song); Andy/Marty start E then A.
- **Justin**: Grade 1 (Modules 0–7: basics → A/D → rhythm/changes → capo/minor → Dm → C → G → consolidation) → Grade 2 (F, power chords, blues, fingerpicking) → Grade 3 → Grades 4–6 (barre chords, CAGED) → Grade 7 (modes). Grades 8–9 not built.
- **Andy**: Free 10-Day Starter Course (L1→L10) → website Beginner Levels 0–9 (tuning → first chords → 3-chord songs → G → progressions → blues → fingerstyle → consolidation) → Intermediate.
- **Marty**: Numbered beginner playlists (Acoustic L1→L5, Electric L1+) + mostly song-by-song.
- **GuitarLessons365**: Free = song-by-song; site/Academy = categorized total-beginner→pro path (Beginner, Rock, Blues, Acoustic, Metal, Classical, Jazz, Theory, Ear Training…).
- **YGA**: 30-Day Beginner Challenge (Day 1→30) → pathways (Beginner→Improver→Intermediate) across skills/masterclasses/theory/songs.
- **Paul Davids**: YouTube topical; paid courses are 7-level roadmaps (Learn/Practice/Play → Next Level → Electric Elevation + Acoustic Adventure + Loop School).
- **Groovy**: No free beginner path; paid Guide = hold guitar → tune → read tabs → essential chords → strumming → first song in 30 min.
- **Music Is Win**: Free = viral/topical; No Cover Charge = 500+ masterclasses (not raw-beginner aimed).
- **Signals**: Free = topical theory; paid = Real Rhythm Guitar L1→L2 + Theory & Songwriting course.

### Pricing patterns (synthesis)
- **Monthly subscriptions: $19.99–$29.99/mo** (NCC $19.99; GL365 $24.99; TrueFire/Guitar Club $29.99; Andy ~$13.95).
- **Annual subscriptions: ~$96–$200/yr** → effective $8–$17/mo (45–73% off).
- **Lifetime/one-time: $45–$270** (Signals, Paul Davids, Marty).
- **Free trials + refund guarantees universal** (7–14 day trials; 30–60 day refunds).
- **Two archetypes**: (a) subscription library (TrueFire, YGA, NCC, GL365, Andy); (b) lifetime course ladder (Paul Davids, Signals, Marty).
- **Free YouTube = top-of-funnel only**; revenue sits on owned platforms. Paid unlocks: structure, tabs/PDFs, interactive players, mentor/human contact, ad-free, member discounts.

## PART B — CHORD-SHAPE TEACHING & VERIFICATION GAP (app-relevant)

**The headline:** Free YouTube is **100% watch-and-copy + self-diagnosis by ear**. NO creator (free OR paid) verifies the student's hands.

- **Even paid apps don't look at you**: JustinGuitar community confirms the app *"does not listen… it's up to you to judge if you did it right"* and Chord Perfect *"is just a timer."* Yousician (audio) is the closest competitor and its #1 complaint is *"it doesn't tell you anything about hand placement or finger position."* Audio says a chord is wrong; only vision says **WHY** — and works **pre-strum**.
- **Chord diagrams = standardized lingua franca**: vertical grid, dots numbered 1–4, X/O row. Justin even teaches the notation first ("How To Read Chord Boxes"). → Your app should report errors IN this notation (color the offending dot red).
- **Mistake taxonomy already exists** (reuse as error classes, don't invent): flat/collapsed fingers muting strings; fingers too far from fret wire (buzz); thumb wrapped/high; wrist not dropped; not enough arm pull; wrong bass string. Justin's F-chord lesson reads like a rubric (thumb, wrist, rotation, lever effect).
- **Camera conventions to match/beat**: baseline = talking-head + mirrored fretting-hand close-up cut between; leaders = GL365 (simultaneous multi-angle), TrueFire (viewer-switchable + fretboard animation + slow-mo). Strumming hand is under-served everywhere — a blind spot you can own.
- **The killer automation target**: Justin's "Chord Perfect Practice" — form chord → pick each string hunting dead notes → reform. Every teacher prescribes it; no product performs it. Your camera feature automates exactly this.
- **Barre chords = highest-value CV target**: failures are geometric (index flat on pad, joint over a string, thumb high, wrist not dropped) — precisely what a camera sees and a mic can't. Ship graded barre scaffolding: mini-F → high-fret barre → fret-1 F, with per-string pass/fail + specific geometric callout.

**No commercial camera-based chord-check product exists** (only research demos: Burns SMC'06, NVIDIA Jetson, hobby GitHub repos). The gap is wide open.

## PART C — STRATEGIC READ FOR A NEW GUITAR APP

1. **Exploitable gaps in incumbents**:
   - Justin's paid stack is **fragmented** (Tabs / app / theory à-la-carte) and hard for beginners to parse. A single clean plan wins.
   - Andy's strength is the **10-day streak loop** + one obvious price. Copy the commitment mechanic.
   - Marty/Groovy win on **song-first instant gratification**. Your app needs a "play a real song in minutes" on-ramp.
   - **Nobody verifies technique visually.** This is the defensible moat — audio competitors (Yousician) are explicitly weak on hand placement.
2. **Pricing to beat**: a clean **~$12–15/mo or ~$99–130/yr** subscription sits between Andy (~$13.95/mo) and TrueFire/Guitar Club ($29.99/mo) — competitive and proven.
3. **Retention to copy**: graded curriculum + "next lesson" pointer (Justin/Andy/YGA), streak/progress bars (Andy/Justin apps), community (Discord/forum), weekly live cadence (Groovy/Andy/YGA).
4. **Curriculum gating**: ship the 8 open chords first in consensus order (E/Em→A→D→Am/Dm→C→G), F/barre as a separately-scaffolded later tier, with **verified-shape gating** (advance only when the camera confirms the shape) — something no incumbent can do.
