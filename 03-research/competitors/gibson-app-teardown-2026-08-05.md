Purpose: fill a gap in the original 2026-08-04 teardown (Yousician/Fender/Justin/Simply).
Gibson App was NOT in that set and is now the closest full-featured incumbent. Sources:
gibson.app/ (landing, 2026-08-05 capture), gibson.app/best-guitar-app (their own 5-way
comparison, "Updated February 2026"), Guitar World / MusicRadar / Premier Guitar quotes
carried on their site, Reddit r/guitarlessons.

## WHAT IT IS
- Launched 2021 (per their comparison page). Brand: Gibson (guitar maker) + Gibson Brands.
- Platforms: iOS + Android. (Our product is iOS-only per spec — note the cross-platform gap.)
- Claims 100,000+ guitarists; 4.6★ / 10K+ ratings (App Store + Google Play).
- 7-day free trial, no credit card. Price ~$19.99/mo OR $129.99/yr (~$10.83/mo).
- Bundle hook: buy a Gibson/Epiphone/Kramer guitar or MESA/Boogie amp → 3 months free.

## LESSON DELIVERY
- Step-by-step expert-designed path (Rhythm vs Lead after basics). Real instructor video.
- "Real-time feedback that actually works": mic listens, analyses pitch + timing AS YOU PLAY,
  highlights the missed note / rhythm mistake and guides correction. (Real-time, in-app — like
  Yousician/Simply, NOT our async record-a-take model.)
- AI personalised post-session reports (since ~2025): complete 5 lessons/day → detailed
  performance report judging progress, areas to improve. THIS is the feature most adjacent to
  our async-critique idea — but it is generated IN-SESSION from real-time play, not a submitted
  take you get back later.
- Skills & Achievements: ~270 tracked discrete skills, data-driven progress viz after each
  session ("fitness tracker for guitar"). Built on "real playing data from over 1M learners."
- Slow-down / loop / note-by-note; Stop & Go (pauses, waits while you find next chord) — iOS only.
- Built-in digital amp + 6 pedals (iOS, wired headphones / iRig). Licensed song library
  (multi-level simplified→original) from named artists. Artist-taught lessons (Ryan Roxie,
  Jared James Nichols, Richie Faulkner). Community forum (2025). Tuner + metronome built in.
- Weekly leagues / rankings for motivation. New songs + courses added weekly.

## WHAT THEY DO NOT DO (vs our wedge)
- NOT async. Feedback is real-time-while-playing; the "AI report" is still in-session, not a
  submitted take returned same-day, unlimited times. This is the gap we exploit.
- NO front-camera chord-shape check (consistent with all other incumbents).
- Filmed-human content (opposite of our zero-filmed-footage / synthetic-avatar decision).
- Song-licensing-heavy — confirms our spec's "no songs in v1" is a real different scope choice,
  not just a licensing dodge; incumbents lean hard on licensed songs for retention.

## PRICING CONTEXT (our $12/mo positioning)
- Gibson $129.99/yr (~$10.83/mo) and $19.99/mo. Fender $149.99/yr (~$12.50/mo). Simply $119.99/yr.
  Yousician $89–140/yr. Our $12/mo sits mid-pack; annualised ($144/yr) is slightly ABOVE Gibson's
  $129.99/yr. Implication: we must justify $12/mo on the async-unlimited feedback wedge, because
  Gibson undercuts us annually while offering more features (amp, songs, community). The moat is
  NOT price — it is the async + unlimited + confidence-honest critique no incumbent offers.

## THREAT ASSESSMENT
- Highest-feature incumbent we've documented. The AI report + 270-skill tracking + real-time
  feedback means "personalised feedback" is no longer a unique claim — only the ASYNC model is.
- Their "90% of guitars unused in year 1" stat is the exact churn problem our product targets;
  we should borrow that framing in positioning, not cede it to Gibson.
- Action for the business's spec/plan: explicitly contrast async-record-a-take vs real-time-
  in-app in the differentiation section. Do not claim "AI feedback" as unique — claim "submit a
  take, get it back same day, unlimited, with honest confidence."
