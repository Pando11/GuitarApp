# Competitive Teardown: JustinGuitar vs Andy Guitar (YouTube guitar lessons)
Research date: 2026-08 (live pages verified where noted). FX used: £1 ≈ $1.27 USD.

---

## 1. JustinGuitar — Justin Sandercoe (@justinguitar, 2M subs, 1.2K videos)

### 1. Teaching style
- Studio-lit talking-head format: Justin seated with guitar, primary head-on mid-shot, cut to an **over-the-shoulder / top-down fretboard cam** and a **close-up strumming-hand cam** whenever a shape or rhythm is demonstrated. Newer uploads use multicam + on-screen chord boxes and TAB overlays.
- Verbal style: calm, unhurried, Australian, heavy use of "why" explanation before "how"; deliberately anti-hype. Explicit named devices in his method ("anchor fingers", "air changes", "THE pattern", "one-minute changes") are recurring vocabulary students learn to recognise.
- Pacing: slow demo → play at speed → slow again; short (4–8 min) legacy lessons, newer content 10–30 min.
- Evidence: module titles on the live Grade 1 page ("A & D Chords: Play Your First Song!", "Air Changes, Dynamics & Consolidation"), and top videos are single-concept chord lessons of 4–8 min.
  - https://www.justinguitar.com/classes/beginner-guitar-course-grade-one

### 2. Lesson setup (typical)
Intro/hook → why this matters → demo the target chord/technique slowly with fretboard close-up → common mistakes ("don't do this") → practice prescription (e.g. one-minute changes, metronome BPM target) → song application → recap + "see you in the next lesson" pointing at the next numbered lesson. Every YouTube lesson has a matching website lesson page with notes, diagrams and a practice item you can add to a practice routine.

### 3. Curriculum order (beginner path)
Live course index (https://www.justinguitar.com/guitar-lessons) shows a graded ladder:
- **Grade 1 (Beginner)** — Module 0 Guitar Basics (tuning, chord boxes, pick hold) → M1 A & D chords + first song → M2 rhythm/chord changes + first riff → M3 capo, minor chords, up-strums → M4 metronome, stretches, "THE pattern", Dm → M5 basic theory, songbook, C chord → M6 6:8 time, G-chord hack, alternate picking → M7 air changes, dynamics, consolidation. (~75 lessons, ~8 hrs.)
- **Grade 2** — F chord, power/rock chords, blues, fingerpicking.
- **Grade 3** — consolidation of beginner skills + practice-craft.
- **Grade 4–6 (Intermediate)** — E-shape then A-shape barre chords, rhythm mastery, transcribing, CAGED, style modules.
- **Grade 7 (Advanced)** — modes, rock technique, country/funk basics. Grades 8–9 explicitly *not yet built* (he redirects to Approved Teachers).
- Parallel tracks: Practical Music Theory (Grades 1–7 modules), Blues Immersion, Strumming SOS, Ear Training, Transcribing.

### 5. Advertising / promotion
- Funnel is YouTube → justinguitar.com free lesson page → app/store. Channel header links justinguitar.com; description pushes "1,300 free video lessons".
- Second channel **JustinGuitar Songs** (1.28M subs) cross-promoted from the main channel shelf — song tutorials as top-of-funnel.
- Store products advertised in-video and via end screens: Lessons & Songs app (built with Musopia), Music Theory app, Note/Time/Ear Trainer apps, Tabs subscription, songbooks/DVDs, in-person Workshops.
- Endorsement social proof used heavily on landing pages (Steve Vai, Mark Knopfler, Brian May), plus TrustPilot review pull-quotes.
- Community posts on YouTube (#KeepPlaying, #JustinGuitarFamily), gear-collab/giveaway videos (e.g. "Play Wish You Were Here & Win Rare Pink Floyd Gifts!", ToneWoodAmp demo) — brand collabs as content.
- Donation-based "JustinGuitar Friends" model is itself the pitch: free content + honour-system donations (avg recurring $25/mo per donor, ~<0.1% donate). https://www.justinguitar.com/donate

### 6. Retention mechanics
- Numbered, graded curriculum with explicit "next lesson" — the single biggest retention device.
- Free account on justinguitar.com: progress tracking, practice-routine builder, saved songbook.
- **Discourse community forum** (community.justinguitar.com) with official moderators/Approved Teachers, per-lesson threads, "Learning Journey" logs.
- Apps with daily practice routines and streak/progress tracking; Tabs library with grade-tagged songs so students always have a next song at their level.
- Monthly live Zoom Music Theory classes; live streams; workshops (in-person events).
- Email list + newsletters; second channel for songs keeps casual viewers in the ecosystem.

### 7. Paid plan — **YES (multiple, à-la-carte; the core beginner course stays free)**
Verified live on justinguitar.com:
- **JustinGuitar Tabs**: 7-day free trial, then **$49.99/year (billed annually, shown as $4.17/mo)**. 790+ chord/lyric sheets, 640+ detailed tabs, auto-scroll, transpose, play-along, full-screen, grade tags, lesson links. https://www.justinguitar.com/store/tabs
- **Lessons & Songs app** (Musopia): free tier + paid subscription; pricing only shown after sign-up. Community/official threads reference a **$129.99 annual** charge and legacy plans around ~$9.99–$11/month, plus a promoted **lifetime access at $209.99 (sale, reg. ~$299.99)**. Treat as approximate — not verifiable on a public page. https://community.justinguitar.com/t/charged-129-99-for-yearly-app-subscription-but-it-doesn-t-show-in-my-account/414349
- **Practical Music Theory**: modules 1–2 free; full course now sold as **lifetime access** (legacy monthly ~$5/mo grandfathered; students report current one-off ≈ $200). https://www.justinguitar.com/store/practical-music-theory
- Other paid: Blues Immersion, Strumming SOS, Solo Blues with MMS, Transcribing Blues Solos, workshops (price varies), Note Trainer/Time Trainer/Ear Trainer apps.
- **Difference vs free YouTube**: YouTube/website lessons (Grades 1–7) are genuinely free forever. Paid buys *tooling and songs*, not core instruction — interactive tabs/play-along, app UX with structured path + progress + daily routines, offline mobile, licensed song library, theory course depth, live classes, and instant-feedback/immersion programs.

## 2. Andy Guitar — Andy Crowley (@andycrowley, 3.14M subs, 2.5K videos)

### 1. Teaching style
- Warm, chatty, high-energy UK (Yorkshire) delivery; "play a song today" framing rather than technique-first.
- Camera: front-facing mid-shot plus frequent **mirrored/over-the-shoulder fretboard inserts** and picture-in-picture close-ups; heavy **on-screen chord boxes, lyric/chord scrolling text and countdown counts-in** so viewers can strum along in real time.
- Lessons are long-ish (12–18 min) and song-outcome driven — his #1 and #2 videos are 16-minute "first lesson" and "ten songs with two chords".
- Newer channel direction adds artist interviews/podcast content ("In The Room With…", Joe Satriani 94K views) alongside lessons.

### 2. Lesson setup (typical)
Hook/promise ("play ten songs with two easy chords") → gear/tuning housekeeping → teach chord shape with close-up → slow strum-along with on-screen chords → **play-along at tempo with backing/count-in** → song application → recap + "see you in lesson 2" + link to the free course page on andyguitar.co.uk (and now membership CTA).

### 3. Curriculum order (beginner path)
- Entry funnel is the **free 10-Day Guitar Starter Course** on YouTube: Lesson 1 "Absolute Beginner? Start Here!" → Lesson 2 easy 2-chord song + lead → Lesson 3 'Three Little Birds' → … 10 daily lessons. Mirrored by a **10-Day Ukulele Starter Course**.
- Website pathway (2025 re-film, membership) is level-numbered 0→9 on the Beginner Acoustic pathway:
  L0 Start Here (tuning, restringing, chord boxes, TAB, note circle, strumming with/without pick) → L1 First Chords, Skills & Songs → L2 Three-Chord Songs & Strumming Patterns → L3 G major & Common Progressions → L4 Most Common Progressions & Strumming → L5 Theory Catch-up + Half-way Consolidation → L6 Beginner Blues/Busker Chords → L7 Acoustic Embellishments & Key of C → L8 Fingerstyle Intro, Minor Keys & A minor Pentatonic → L9 Beginner Acoustic Consolidation. Then separate Intermediate pathway (incl. Genre Studies L7, Acoustic Lead Guitar).
  - https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/courses/ , https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/courses/absolute-beginner-guitar

### 5. Advertising / promotion
- Channel header links **andyguitar.co.uk/courses** + 2 more links; **"Join" (YouTube channel membership)** button enabled, and a "Members only" videos tab — YouTube memberships run in parallel with the site membership.
- Video descriptions carry direct membership CTAs ("Join here ▷ https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/membership").
- Series branding as advertising: everything is labelled "[10 Day Guitar Starter Course]" so the playlist sells itself.
- Artist-interview collabs (Joe Satriani, The Darkness, Neck Deep, ALT BLK ERA, Dinosaur Pile-Up) = reach borrowing; Gibson-linked in-person workshops/meet-ups pictured on the membership page.
- Social proof: "Trusted by 3 million YouTube subscribers", guest-teacher collaborations, testimonial wall on course pages.

### 6. Retention mechanics
- Day-numbered "10-Day" course creates a **streak/commitment loop** — the strongest hook in his top videos.
- Website pathways with **0% → 100% progress bars** per course and sequential levels.
- **Discord community** for members; **weekly live Q&A / live streams with Andy** (scheduled recurring appointment = habit).
- Interactive TAB with slow-down/looping/fretboard diagrams keeps practice inside his platform.
- Guest-teacher courses add breadth so members don't churn out for niche topics; 60-day money-back guarantee reduces cancel anxiety; 10-day free trial mirrors the free course habit.
- YouTube channel membership + members-only video tab for viewers who won't leave YouTube.

### 7. Paid plan — **YES: "Access All Areas" membership** (verified live, https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/membership/)
- **Monthly: £10.99/month (~$13.95 USD)**
- **Annual: £99.99/year (~$127 USD), advertised as £8.33/mo, "save 24%"**
- **10-day free trial; 60-day money-back guarantee; cancel anytime.**
- Included: full access to all courses; **800+ videos exclusive to AAA members**; the **completely re-filmed 2025 Beginner Course (exclusive to the site, not on YouTube)**; weekly live Q&A with Andy; Discord community; Interactive TAB (slow-down, loop, fretboard diagrams); guest-teacher courses.
- **Difference vs free YouTube**: YouTube keeps the legacy 10-Day Starter Course, one-off song tutorials and interviews. The membership is where the *new, structured, sequential* curriculum lives (Levels 0–9 + intermediate pathways), plus interactive practice tooling, live human access, community, and progress tracking — i.e. Andy paywalls the curriculum itself, unlike Justin who paywalls tooling/songs.
- Separately: YouTube channel membership tier(s) via the "Join" button (price set in YouTube UI, small monthly fee; unlocks a members-only video tab).

## Head-to-head summary
| | JustinGuitar | Andy Guitar |
|---|---|---|
| Subs | 2M (+1.28M Songs channel) | 3.14M |
| Signature funnel | Graded free curriculum (G1–G7) on web | Free 10-Day Starter Course on YouTube |
| Top video | D Chord first lesson, ~17M | Lesson 1 Start Here, ~38M |
| Tone | Methodical, theory-aware, "why first" | Fast-to-a-song, chatty, play-along |
| Core curriculum | **Free forever** (donations) | **Paywalled** (new 2025 course, members only) |
| Paid | Tabs $49.99/yr; app sub (~$129.99/yr reported, ~$9.99–11/mo legacy; lifetime $209.99 sale); PMT lifetime (~$200); courses/workshops | AAA membership £10.99/mo (~$13.95) or £99.99/yr (~$127); 10-day trial; 60-day guarantee |
| Community | Discourse forum + live theory classes | Discord + weekly live Q&A |

**Strategic read:** Justin monetises *tools and songs* around free instruction and leans on an honour-system donation base + app partnership (Musopia). Andy monetises *the curriculum itself* with a single, simple, trial-led subscription and uses YouTube's 10-day streak course as the acquisition engine. Andy's price point is one clean number; Justin's stack is fragmented and harder for a beginner to parse — a real gap a competitor can exploit with one obvious plan.
