=== COST MODEL (verified, architecture-dependent) ===

Real-time jam: JackTrip Virtual Studio API, per-musician-minute ($0.29–0.67/musician-hour, ~$11.20/session at 20 people, ~$135/mo at 3x/week). Jamulus is free software but you own the ops. Latency threshold ~30ms; local/same-city reliable, cross-country loose-only.
