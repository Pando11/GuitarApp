VALIDATION INTERVIEW SCRIPT — guitar app
Length: 20-25 min. Record with permission. Interviewee ID: ___
Date: ___  Level: beginner (<12mo) / intermediate-plateau / other
How recruited: Reddit / Discord / local shop / guitarist's student / other

GROUND RULES FOR THE INTERVIEWER:
- Ask the question, then shut up. Silence makes them fill it with truth.
- Never explain the app first. Their answers must be uncontaminated.
- When they say something vague ("it was frustrating"), follow up with
  "tell me about the last time that happened — walk me through it."
- Write down exact quotes, especially anything about quitting or paying.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. When you quit a guitar app (or lessons), what was the exact moment you
   stopped? Not the reason you tell people — the actual last session.
   [Hunting the real churn trigger.]

2. Walk me through your last practice session, start to finish. How long,
   what you played, what you used (app? YouTube? book?), how it ended.
   [Behavior, not opinion.]

3. Have you ever paid for Yousician, Gibson, Simply Guitar, Fender Play,
   or lessons? What made you cancel — or if you never paid, what stopped
   you?

4. If an app could LISTEN to your take and tell you exactly what to fix —
   note accuracy, chord correctness, timing — same day, as many times as
   you want, would you pay $12/month for that? Why / why not?
   [This is the v1 value prop — the real gate question.]
4b. (v2 probe, NON-BLOCKING) Separate from the above: would seeing your
   fretting hand on camera to catch finger placement help you learn? Note
   interest, but this does NOT gate v1.

5. (v2 probe, OPTIONAL — does NOT block v1) If they have a stand and are
   comfortable: "Prop your phone so its camera sees your fretting hand for
   a minute." Observe friction only — log it as a v2 data point, not a gate.
   [Camera was the OLD kill criterion; it is now deferred and non-blocking.]

6. Would you want to play with strangers online, or does that make you
   anxious? Have you ever done a remote jam?

7. What song made you pick up the guitar? Would you quit an app that
   couldn't teach it to you?

8. Roughly what have you spent on learning guitar in the last year —
   apps, lessons, books, gear for learning? What would $12/month have to
   beat to be worth it?

9. Do you practice at a consistent time? What interrupts you?

10. If you downloaded a free guitar tuner from a brand, and it worked
    great, would that make you more likely to trust their paid lessons?

11. DEMO GATE (show both, order randomized per interview):
    Clip A: avatar-led synthetic lesson (cartoon coach + fretboard diagram)
    Clip B: human-led lesson clip (any good YouTube lesson excerpt)
    "Which of these would you actually learn from for a month? Which
    would you pay for? Be honest — you're helping me not waste a year."
    Result: chose A / chose B / mixed — notes:
